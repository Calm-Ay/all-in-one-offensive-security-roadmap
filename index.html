HOW TO USE THIS MANUAL
Principle 01
Pick a lane, not everything
Foundations → Web → Active Directory is the highest-ROI beginner path. This is a map, not a novel — jump to a lane and build the skill with your hands. Don't read it front to back.
Principle 02
Free-first
Every entry has a free, legal way in. Official cert pages are listed so you know the target you're working toward — not because you must pay to learn. Pay for nothing but the exam.
Principle 03
Learn by doing
For every concept, find the matching free lab (HTB / THM / PortSwigger) and the matching write-up (IppSec / 0xdf). Watch, then do it yourself with no hints.
Principle 04
Certs = exam + paper, not secret knowledge
Almost everything taught in expensive courses is re-taught, free, by the community. Keep your own methodology repo — the notes you write beat any course PDF. Pay for a cert when you need the credential.

Rules of engagement: everything here is a legitimate, publicly-available learning resource — official sites, open-source repos, free labs, and creators' own published work. Only ever test systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Use isolated VMs for malware. The goal is to become the professional who gets hired to break in.
